The material, and what a
generator has to deliver

Problem statement
The data

Eighty X-ray tomograms of carbon-fibre laminate coupons, three panel families, 25 µm voxels. Each voxel carries one of three labels — material, pore, air outside the specimen. Splits are by panel, never by coupon: sibling coupons of one panel share microstructure and would leak. Patches touching the drilled registration holes are dropped — they read as zero porosity while being almost pure air.

The unit

Training sees 64³ voxel patches — a 1.6 mm cube. A ply is about 19.6 voxels thick, so one patch spans roughly 3.3 plies: the stacking sequence is visible inside a patch, which is exactly what has to be controllable. Two million patches at stride 32.

What a useful generator must do
  • Deliver a requested porosity locally, not only on average
  • Reproduce the ply orientation profile through the thickness
  • Put the specimen boundary where it was asked for
  • Never place air inside the material envelope
  • Accept stacking sequences and shapes never seen in training
  • Produce volumes far larger than any training patch

Three stages, one contract
between them

Overview
Why a latent

Diffusion on 64³ voxels is eight times more elements per step, and the model would spend its capacity on grey-level texture the autoencoder already reproduces. The latent is spatial, not flat: 16³ cells of 8 channels, one cell per 100 µm cube.

Why the VAE is frozen afterwards

Every latent in the store was produced by one checkpoint. The launcher compares the checkpoint the store recorded with the one the run names and refuses to start on a mismatch: decoding with a different VAE is silently wrong, not loudly wrong.

Where the difficulty is

Not in generating one patch. In generating ten thousand of them that agree with each other, with a request painted over the whole volume, at a size that never fits in memory at once. That is what stage 2’s sampler is for.

Stage 1 — what the autoencoder compresses

VAE · the contract
Why air is its own class

A pore and the space outside the coupon are both dark and both empty. With a single binary mask the model had no way to say which one it meant, and exterior air became an unexplained dark region it had to invent. A labelled air class is also what later lets the sampler be told where the specimen ends.

The heads are not symmetric

The grey head emits an intensity on the same scale as the scan — applying a sigmoid to it squashes the output into [0.5, 0.73] and destroys contrast. The class head genuinely is a logit. One decode helper per head, so the two cannot drift apart.

The asymmetry that matters

The encoder sees the labels; the sampler never will. That is deliberate: the latent has to carry the segmentation, so the denoiser does not have to generate it twice and cannot contradict itself.

How the autoencoder is trained

Choosing the latent width

Six rungs were trained on the same data and the same trunk — z ∈ {32, 16, 8, 4, 2, 1} channels per cell. The rung is chosen on four things at once: porosity error, pore Dice in the dense regime, air Dice, and the tile-seam ratio — not on total validation loss, which is not comparable across compression factors.

Structure saturates: below a certain width the pore Dice falls away, above it nothing more is bought. The chosen rung is the smallest that has already saturated.

A trap worth naming

This decoder is BatchNorm. Freezing a module means eval(), not only requires_grad_(False) — running statistics are not gradients, and a train-mode forward moves them. A “frozen” encoder whose statistics drift silently invalidates the entire latent store built from it.

The material map is the envelope, not the pores

It pools label ≠ air — pores count as specimen. Pooling the pore class instead would be a pore map at 100 µm, and the model would learn to upsample it rather than generate porosity. The distinction decides whether the network invents microstructure or copies it.

Train on samples, not on means

Latents are drawn z = μ + σ·ε per batch, and so are the neighbour latents. Conditioning on posterior means while regressing a posterior sample leaves the neighbour input short of E[σ²] per cell — a gap that does not exist at generation time, where every neighbour is a real latent.

Two porosities, and they are not the same number

Full-patch φ = pore / 64³, air outside the specimen included in the denominator — what the store records, because the conditioning has to describe the patch the encoder actually saw. Material porosity = pore / material — what an engineer asks for. The sampler converts; the store never does.

Stage 2 — the diffusion process,
in the latent

Forward & reverse
The schedule

Cosine, T = 1000. āₓ comes straight from the cosine f, so its terminal value is already zero to float precision — there is no signal leak at the end of the ladder to close.

What the network regresses

Not the clean latent. A quantity from which the clean latent can be recovered in closed form — and which quantity turns out to matter at the last step. Next slide.

Why DDIM

Deterministic given the initial noise, and it skips the ladder: 50 calls instead of 1000. Every random draw in the sampler is made once and reused, never sampled per step.

ε or v — the last step
decides it

Objective
TargetDefinitionRecovering the clean latent
εthe noise that was added x̂₀ = (xₜ − √(1−ā) ε̂) / √ā
vv = √ā ε − √(1−ā) x₀ x̂₀ = √ā xₜ − √(1−ā) v̂ — no division
The failure, concretely

At the terminal step of this cosine schedule √āₔ ≈ 6 × 10⁻¹⁷. The ε form then divides the network output by that number: the recovered latent comes back of order 10⁸ and the clamp that guards the reverse process saturates. Every sampling chain therefore began from a clamp artefact rather than a prediction — on the first step, where the whole trajectory is decided.

The v form recovers the same quantity with no division and stays of order one. The schedule object refuses to combine an exactly-zero terminal signal with the ε target, because there the division is by exactly zero.

Not the usual argument

The published motivation for zero terminal SNR (Lin et al. 2024) attacks schedules that build ā as a cumulative product of clipped β, which leaves real signal at t = T that the model sees in training but never at sampling. That leak does not exist here — ā is taken straight from the cosine. The rescale moves it by less than 10⁻¹⁶. We adopt v for the numerical reason above, and say so, rather than borrowing a justification that does not apply.

One reverse process, not one per parameterisation

Nothing downstream branches on the objective. The schedule names the training target, converts an output back either way, and the DDIM step is written on the pair (x̂₀, ε̂). Guidance is combined on the raw network outputs: at fixed t the map between v and ε is affine with shared coefficients, so an affine combination of guidance arms commutes with it. The conversion happens once, inside the step.

A bug in this family is invisible in the loss curve and fatal in the sample. Both are worth stating in a paper.

Standardised log(φ+10⁻³) with a learned null token for guidance; relative depth; the six per-face distances through one small network over the whole vector; and an availability-masked pool of the neighbour latents.

No global porosity input

It is redundant once a per-patch field fixes the volume mean, and it was the source of a local/global conflation bug. Volume-level control is carried by the field, not by a scalar.

The design rule

Conditioning must be composable, never categorical.

A one-hot for “specimen family” can only ever reproduce the three materials in the dataset. A continuous orientation field makes an unseen stacking sequence a recombination of parts that were seen. That is the whole reason the model can be asked for a layup that is not in the data.

Six distances, not one

A single distance-to-surface scalar cannot say which side the specimen ends on, so a patch under the top face and a patch against a side wall asked for the same thing. Six per-face gaps, capped and normalised, are one definition shared by the dataset builder and the sampler.

Orientation: pool the components

Never the angle, and never renormalise the pooled vector. The nominal ply sequence is used, not measured deviations — a measured residual is defined relative to the volume mean and would be identically zero in a unidirectional laminate.

The trick that makes joint
sampling possible

Neighbours — sampled, then noised
01

Draw, do not read

Each existing neighbour is sampled from its own stored posterior, μ + σ·ε, exactly as the target is drawn.

02

Noise it to its own timestep

With probability ½ the target’s own t, otherwise uniform below it. The level tₙₛ goes in as a sinusoidal embedding.

03

Sometimes remove all six

With probability 0.1 every face goes UNKNOWN at level zero. That is the guidance null arm, so training and sampling share one definition of “no neighbour information”.

At generation time a neighbour is never clean. A denoiser trained only on clean neighbours meets an input distribution it has never seen on its very first sampling step.

In the previous generation of this model that is exactly what happened, and the joint sampler had to drop neighbour conditioning altogether — which made its neighbour guidance scale inert. Noised neighbours in training are what buy the joint mode.

One training step

LDM · latent objective + decoded auxiliary
Why score the decode at all

The latent objective is blind to any error that leaves the residual unchanged. Air placed inside the specimen, a delivered porosity that ignores the request, and dark regions labelled material are exactly that: two latents can differ in all three and carry the same residual.

Why only the lowest quartile of t

A recovered latent from a high-t step is a blur with no pore structure in it, so every term would be measuring the schedule instead of the model. A hard cap on decoded items keeps the memory bounded — a live autograd graph through the decoder costs a few hundred MB per item.

Frozen means frozen

The VAE in this graph carries no gradient and runs in eval mode. Otherwise a decode in train mode folds generated latents into the frozen model’s running statistics — and the clean latent is deliberately not clamped here, so the gradient survives on the items that are most wrong.

Part three · the core of the method
Patch, tile, chunk —
and one joint
reverse process

Three words that are all “64 voxels” and none of which mean the same thing. Getting them apart is what lets a model trained on 1.6 mm cubes write a 25 mm laminate.

Three grids with three jobs

A patch is content. A tile is an address. A window is a network call. A chunk is one reverse process.

Joint denoising: fuse the
prediction, not the sample

Inside one chunk
Why this is not tiled generation

Blending finished samples averages two independent draws. That halves the variance exactly where two patches meet, and a region with the wrong variance is off-manifold for the decoder — it decodes as a smooth band, which is what a seam looks like. Fusing the prediction keeps one trajectory, with the variance the schedule asks for.

Guidance is nested, not symmetric

out(∅) + sᵧ[out(φ)−out(∅)] + sₙ[out(φ,nb)−out(φ)]. Porosity and neighbours are correlated, and symmetric guidance on correlated conditions drifts off-manifold; nested measures neighbour guidance given porosity.

The two extremes are the same code

A chunk of one tile is sequential generation; one chunk covering the volume is pure joint denoising. No second implementation, no switch — only a chunk size.

Where a window’s six
faces come from

Neighbour sources at generation time
The rule that makes a boundary work

A finished chunk is not handed over clean. It is re-noised to the current timestep with fresh noise before it is read as a neighbour. That is the same rule as inpainting by repeated re-noising, and it is why the model — which was trained on neighbours at their own noise levels — recognises the input.

UNKNOWN is not a special case

It is the exact null the training step used when it dropped all six neighbours at once, so “no information about that face” means the same thing in training and in sampling.

Noise belongs to the request

Every random draw is one field the size of the canvas, rolled so that the value used at a canvas position is the one the request sees there. Moving a request inside a larger canvas moves its noise with it — without that, changing the assembly grid also changed the sample, and the two effects could not be separated.

One generation, end to end

The loop
01

Snap the request to whole tiles

A size is given in millimetres and snapped to a whole number of 64-voxel tiles on every axis. The snap is checked, not assumed: a request that would silently lose a tile is refused.

02

Paint the request

A porosity per tile, a material fraction per latent cell, and an orientation profile from the layup. All three are computable before the first network call.

03

Cut into chunks, raster order

The last chunk on an axis is simply smaller. Nothing about the model changes with the number of chunks.

04

Per chunk: noise the canvas

Drawn once, in the frame of the request.

05

Per timestep: read, predict, fuse, step

Build every window’s six faces from the four sources; batch the windows; three guidance calls; fuse the predictions with the cosine weight; take one DDIM step on the canvas.

06

The chunk is finished

It joins the pool of finished chunks and is re-noised on demand for whichever chunk comes next.

07

Decode overlapped, blend, argmax

The last stage, and the one that used to leave a visible seam of its own.

Cost, and where it goes

Network calls scale as chunks × steps × windows × 3. Windows per chunk grow with the chunk volume at stride 32, so a bigger chunk is more coherent and more expensive in the same breath. Memory is set by the chunk canvas and the window batch — never by the volume.

The decode has its own seam

Overlapped decode & blending order
Blend the evidence, then decide

The grey level and the raw three-class logits are accumulated under the tapered window, and the argmax is taken after the blend. Taking the argmax first and blending labels averages decisions instead of evidence — near a boundary that produces a label that neither decoder window actually predicted.

Two seam periods, one baseline

Discontinuity is measured at the window period (64 voxels) and at the chunk period (64 × chunk tiles), both against one interior slice-to-slice baseline. A gap between the two says which mechanism is at fault: window overlap, or the chunk boundary. Reporting a single seam number cannot distinguish them.

A measured lesson

Part of what looked like a diffusion seam was the decoder’s tiling all along. The overlapped decode removed it — on real volumes as well as generated ones, which is how we know it was the decode and not the prior.

Asking for something specific

Three painted fields
The average is on raw fractions

A window’s requested porosity is the tile field averaged over its own footprint, each tile weighted by the volume it covers — on raw pore fractions, before the log transform. The mean of a log is not the log of a mean. Sampling only the tile under the window centre handed the two windows on either side of a step the two extremes, and reproduced the requested step as a wider, offset one.

Then converted to what the model was trained on

The request is a material porosity; the store holds full-patch φ. Per window the request is scaled by the window’s own material fraction — before the clip and before the log, because clipping first turns a legal request into an illegal one and a log turns a scale into an offset.

Why a painted envelope buys shape control

The envelope is an input, not an output. Asking for a curved specimen, a notch or a drilled hole is a different painting of the same channel — no retraining, and the model has a labelled air class to put outside it.

Why it extends to arbitrary size

Scaling
What grows

The number of chunks — linearly in volume — and with it wall time and disk. Nothing else. A larger volume is more of the same work, never a different kind of work.

What does not

GPU memory, the model, the receptive field, and the conditioning contract. Every long-range constraint travels in a painted field — porosity, envelope, orientation — not through the network’s receptive field. The only cross-chunk communication is the six-face neighbour channel.

Where it strains — honestly

Raster order gives the volume a preferred direction: a chunk is conditioned on finished neighbours behind it and on UNKNOWN ahead. Any bias in the blend therefore accumulates along that direction, and the residual discontinuity we can still measure sits at the chunk period, not the window period. That is the thread the last part of this talk pulls.

How a generated volume
is checked

Evaluation design
The principle

Every metric has a real-volume floor.

The same statistic is computed three ways: generated against real, real against real, and the ratio between them. Two crops of the same laminate do not agree perfectly either — a distance with no floor cannot tell a model failure from the natural spread of the material.

And a manifest per volume

Model run, step, objective, sampler geometry, the request, the commit. A metric declares the fields it reads and refuses a volume whose manifest does not carry them. A number no one can trace back to a checkpoint is not a result.

01
Does it obey, locally?

Delivered against requested porosity within one volume, tile by tile — not the between-volume correlation, which is far easier and was reported once by mistake.

02
Seams, at both periods

Window period and chunk period, on the grey level and on the pore log-odds, against one interior baseline.

03
Geometry control

Air outside the requested envelope and inside it; surface position and roughness; a notch, a hole, and a sphere — a curved specimen no training patch contains.

04
Microstructure distances

Two-point correlation, pore-size distribution, border-corrected Ripley’s K, slice FID — each floored by matched real pairs.

05
Did it memorise?

Nearest-neighbour search against the store the volumes were generated from, at the stride that makes rows unique.

Part five · where we would like your help
Every seam in this
system is an average
in latent space

And nothing in a KL-regularised latent promises that an average of two valid codes is itself a valid code.

Three places we blend —
and one shared assumption

The problem
A — window overlap

Up to eight predictions are combined per voxel, every step, inside every chunk. A weighted mean of network outputs.

B — chunk boundary

A finished chunk is re-noised and read as a neighbour. The junction between two independently completed reverse processes.

C — the decode

Grey levels and class logits accumulate under a taper before the argmax. A weighted mean of decoder outputs.

All three are weighted averages taken in a space that was never trained to be averaged.

A KL-VAE latent is regularised only by a penalty on the marginal. It says nothing about the straight line between two encodings. Where that line leaves the region the decoder was trained on, the blend does not fail loudly — it decodes as something smooth and plausible and slightly wrong, which is exactly the signature of a seam. And it is worst in the overlap regions, which is to say: worst everywhere two things have to agree.

The symptom we can point at

The window-period discontinuity is essentially closed — overlapping windows and a tapered decode deal with it. What is left sits at the chunk period, the one junction where two completed reverse processes meet, and it is the one that repeats once per chunk boundary in a large volume. That is a blending symptom, not a capacity symptom: the same model, on the same request, produces it only where two results have to be reconciled.

The proposal: make the
blend operator well posed

VRRAE — where we would put it
What the VRRAE bottleneck gives

A truncated SVD of the batch latent matrix; the coefficients used directly as the posterior mean, with the identity map that the ablation showed is necessary; the KL applied to those coefficients. Because the right singular vectors are orthonormal, the coefficients are regularised without a penalty and ordered by importance, and posterior collapse is bounded to ±s̄ᵢ/√N rather than to wherever a nonlinear encoder happens to land.

The reported gains are on interpolation and random generation — not on reconstruction. That is the axis this pipeline is limited on.

What we would use it for

Not to replace the spatial latent wholesale. To give the three blends above a space in which an average is a legal code. Project the overlapping predictions onto the fixed basis extracted after training, combine the coefficients there, and reconstruct. An interpolation of coefficients on an orthonormal basis stays inside the span the decoder was trained on, by construction rather than by hope.

Why that matters most at
size, and on shapes we
never trained on

The argument
01 — the error compounds

A one-chunk volume has no chunk boundary. A coupon-scale volume has tens of them, arranged in a raster order, each conditioned on the ones before it. Any bias in the blend is applied again at every junction and in the same direction.

A blend that is on-manifold by construction does not remove a constant error — it removes the term that grows with the number of chunks. That is the term that decides whether the method has a size limit.

02 — unseen CFRP geometry

The requests we care about are the ones we cannot show the model: a curved surface, a notch, a drilled hole, a stacking sequence that no coupon in the dataset has.

At exactly those places the material map is fractional, several faces are out of bounds, and the latent sits furthest from anything the encoder ever produced. A rank-limited representation cannot express a direction outside the training span — so the failure mode changes from “an off-manifold artefact” to “the nearest plausible microstructure”. For an engineering deliverable that is the failure we want.

03 — it can be added late

The fixed basis is extracted once after training and then applied per sample. In the weakest version of this proposal the denoiser does not change at all: the rank-reduced space is used only as the space in which overlaps and boundaries are combined.

That makes it testable against the current model, with the current evaluation suite, as an A/B on the blend operator alone — the seam ratio at the chunk period is already the measurement that would show it.

A clean experiment: same checkpoint, same noise, same request. Only the blend changes.

What we already tried,
and what it cost us

A documented negative result
The 2026 attempt

We replaced the whole spatial bottleneck with a flat VRRAE one: flatten the encoder output, one fully-connected layer to a target dimension, then the published rank-reduction layer, then the reparameterisation and the KL — following the identity-mean construction. The decoder was made grey-only to isolate the bottleneck from the segmentation loss balance.

Reconstruction came out about three times worse in L1 than the spatial baseline. We then ran the controlled ablation — identical flat architecture, the rank-reduction layer replaced by learned linear heads, everything else held fixed — to separate the two candidate causes. The direction was closed as a negative result for bottleneck replacement.

Two accepted consequences we walked into

A flat latent also invalidates the 3-D denoiser and its neighbour conditioning — the whole diffusion stage would have had to be redesigned for a token representation. And the discoverable rank is bounded by the batch size, so latent width and batch size stop being independent choices.

What we think actually went wrong
  • Capacity, not method. A few hundred numbers for a 1.6 mm cube of stochastic texture, against roughly eight thousand in the spatial baseline. Porosity is not a smooth low-order field; it is a random medium.
  • A basis that rotates per batch. Estimated in a very high-dimensional feature space, with near-degenerate singular values and the gradient instability that comes with them; and the training-time bases are not the single basis used at evaluation.
  • We measured the wrong axis. We judged it on reconstruction. The published advantage is on interpolation and sampling.
So the question we would bring to you is not “does a VRRAE bottleneck reconstruct XCT?”. It is: can rank reduction give a 3-D spatial latent a blend operator that stays on the manifold?

What we would need to
answer together

Open questions
Q1
Rank along which axis?

Channels within a latent cell, the flattened patch, or the whole chunk canvas. Each gives a different object: a per-cell code book, a per-patch code, or a global field basis. Only the first two keep the spatial structure the denoiser needs.

Q2
Does the batch-basis argument survive?

The consistency result assumes enough linearly independent samples per batch. If the “samples” are spatial cells of one volume they are strongly correlated by construction. Does the fixed basis still hold — and should it be extracted per panel family or once globally?

Q3
Bottleneck, or operator?

Train the VAE with the rank-reduced bottleneck, or leave the trained latent alone and apply rank reduction only inside the blend. The second is cheap and testable now; the first is the stronger claim.

Q4
How is interpolation quality measured inside a microstructure?

There is no standard metric. Our proposal: two-point correlation, pore-size distribution and Ripley’s K evaluated across the seam, each floored by matched real pairs cut from the same panel. That floor is already built.

Q5
What is the shared publication?

One reading: a rank-reduced latent-blending operator for tiled and chunked latent diffusion, demonstrated on a real industrial 3-D material where the seam is measurable against a real-volume floor — method from your side, the volumetric problem, pipeline and evaluation from ours.

What we can put on the table

A trained three-class VAE and a conditional 3-D latent diffusion model on real aerospace CFRP; a chunked joint sampler with a fixed geometry contract; an evaluation suite with real-volume floors and per-volume manifests; and an A/B harness where only the blend operator changes.
