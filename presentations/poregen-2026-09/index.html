Why build a generator for CFRP microstructure

00 · Motivation
Aerospace CFRP parts are inspected by X-ray CT. Porosity is the defect that decides whether a part is accepted. The models that measure it need training data that almost nobody has.

The data problem

Porous specimens must be manufactured on purpose. Scanning is slow, labelling is manual, and the porosity range in any real campaign is narrow and unbalanced.

Volumes available78
Volume φ range0.002 – 0.107

The ask

A generator you can drive: name a porosity level and a stacking sequence, and receive a volume with that porosity, that ply layout, and realistic pore morphology.

Control 1local + global φ
Control 2ply orientation θ(z)

The constraint

A specimen scan is roughly 1024×1024×1000 voxels. Full-volume 3D diffusion does not fit in memory, so generation must be patch-based — and the patches must agree with each other.

Patch64³
Assemblytiled, no blending

The through-line of this talk: every design decision came from a measurement, and most of the interesting ones came from a measurement that showed the previous decision was wrong.

What Naiff establishes

A KL-regularised VAE plus diffusion produces high-fidelity binary porous media, and scalar porosity conditioning implicitly carries permeability, TPCF and pore-size distribution. This is the domain precedent for choosing KL over VQ.

What it leaves open

No spatial control inside the volume, no assembly beyond one 256³ cube, no cross-patch consistency mechanism, no fibre-reinforced composite — where the microstructure has a direction that rock does not.

The gap all five share

Every one of them either generates a single fixed-size volume, or assembles larger ones by blending decoded patches. None conditions a patch on what its neighbours already contain. Why this matters for PoreGen

If masked diffusion is implicitly a learned-order autoregressive model, then a fixed deterministic order — checkerboard parity — is a principled special case, not an ad-hoc simplification. That is the licence for D23: train only the two neighbour states that inference will actually meet.

The lesson learned the hard way: an order is only "independent within a phase" relative to a patch spacing. Order and spacing must be reasoned about together.

Latent design: what the literature argues

01 · Literature

KL or VQ?

MAR measures a clear gap at the same tokeniser stride: KL-16 reaches FID 3.50 against VQ-16 at 7.82 with an identical diffusion loss. Pinaya argues the opposite for near-binary MRI. Naiff — same domain as us — uses KL.

PoreGen · D1KL

Collapse and blur

Zhao 2017 explains why the ELBO's KL term makes VAEs blur. Free-bits puts a floor under each channel so it cannot satisfy KL by going silent. VRRAE later proves collapse is structurally bounded in an SVD bottleneck.

PoreGen · D3β 0.05 + free-bits

Beyond per-voxel loss

Larsen 2016 and Rombach 2022 use a discriminator to push past the per-pixel ceiling. Naiff shows reconstruction loss alone is enough for porous media. We started with Naiff's position and later moved to Larsen's.

PoreGen · D16PatchGAN added
The rank-reduction branch — and why it was worth reading

RRAE replaces the KL term with a truncated SVD on the bottleneck: no divergence weight, no free-bits floor, and an adaptive variant that discovers its own rank. VRRAE adds probabilistic sampling on top and proves the collapse bound. It is the most principled alternative to the hyperparameter tuning that D3 and D20 spend their time on — which is exactly why we tried it, and why the negative result on slide 13 is worth reporting.

Evaluation protocol inherited

Gayon-Lombardo and SliceGAN set the standard for microstructure: two-point correlation S₂(r), pore-size distribution, physical transport. Naiff adds slice-FID and Wasserstein-1 on PSD. PoreGen follows this lineage and adds seam discontinuity, which none of them need.

Normalisation — why not z-score (D12)

The pore intensity component sits at −2.96σ under per-volume z-score, so any standard ±3σ clip deletes the pore signal entirely. Every volume saturates the uint8 ceiling in the solid matrix, so a global x / 255 is equivalent to per-volume max-normalisation with no per-volume statistics and no clip.

The mask is a function of the image

Sauvola's threshold is a deterministic function of the local intensity mean and variance. The mask therefore adds no information the XCT does not already carry — which is the argument that later removes it from the encoder input (D14) and keeps it only as a supervision target.

Caveat

Four patches in one volume carried φ > 1.0 from a summed-area-table overflow and are dropped at load time. Voxel size is not recorded in the dataset metadata — it has cost time twice, and the ply pitch still cannot be reported in µm from the data alone.

Consequence 1 — the primary metric

At f=4 a 2-voxel pore maps to about half a latent cell. Individual pore positions are below latent resolution by construction, so Dice is capped around 0.2–0.3 for a correct model. The success criterion is therefore porosity-MAE < 0.005, roughly a tenth of the mean φ of 0.055.

Consequence 2 — neighbours are mandatory

S₂(r) at r=30 sits 4.73× above φ², and the spectral peak of inter-cluster spacing is 42.7 voxels. Pore clusters are larger than one patch. A patch-independent model would produce plausible local density and wrong global structure.

Intensity structure

A 3-component GMM describes the histogram: pores at 37.65, an interface mode at 188.0, solid CFRP at 209.61 with 83.6% of the mass. Global mean 191.66, σ 52.02. Pore/matrix overlap coefficient is 0.045 — well separated in intensity, but the best achievable threshold Dice is only 0.22 because the pores are sub-voxel.

Caveat

Exterior air is not labelled as porosity anywhere in this pipeline. Every analysis built on the mask alone is blind to it. That blind spot survives untouched until slide 31, where it turns out to be the largest open problem in the project.

Why drop the mask from the encoder

The mask is a deterministic Sauvola function of local XCT intensity and variance, so in principle it adds nothing. PCA agrees: XCT alone and XCT+mask both give k@90% = 9. It stays as a supervision target, not an input.

The plateau that would not move

XCT Charbonnier settles at ≈0.030 from step 2000 in every run, R00 through R03, regardless of architecture, schedule or latent width. A per-voxel loss cannot tell a sharp reconstruction from a blurry one with the same mean error.

A split bug worth remembering

R00–R02 showed val porosity-MAE below 0.001 against test at 0.021–0.031. Not overfitting — the splits were not stratified by porosity, so val and test described structurally different volumes. Fixed before R03.

Caveat

R03's error is false-positive dominated: the mask head hallucinates pores when it has to segment from XCT-only latent features. That is what D17's focal loss is aimed at on the next slide.

The VRRAE branch — and why it closed

03 · First stagenegative result
Replace the KL bottleneck with a truncated-SVD rank-reduction layer: no β to tune, no free-bits floor, and a published proof that posterior collapse is structurally bounded rather than arbitrary.
What went wrong — mechanically

Encoder gradient norm: median 873, maximum 4.2×10⁷, drifting upward window over window, while the decoder stayed flat at 0.08–0.3 the whole run. Suspect: the SVD backward's 1/(sᵢ² − sⱼ²) term with near-degenerate singular values.

Two real bugs found on the way

The decoder was fed raw SVD coefficients instead of Y = U·α, and the vendored SVD backward was wrong. Fixing both moved reconstruction from 0.121 to 0.082. A misread of mu_n_active ≈ 0 as posterior collapse also sent a whole run chasing free-bits — the coefficients are simply ordered by singular value.

The ablation that settled it — D26

Same flat bottleneck, same dimensions, same everything, with the batch-SVD layer swapped for plain Linear heads. Linear reached 0.0784 against SVD's 0.1058 — about 26% better. The flat latent was not the limit; the batch-SVD machinery was.

Why the design was structurally awkward for this problem

The discoverable rank is bounded by min(batch, L), so latent width and batch size stop being independent knobs. The basis is re-estimated per batch in a high-dimensional feature space, so it rotates during training and then mismatches the finalised basis used at evaluation. And a flat latent deletes the 3D spatial structure that the neighbour and orientation conditioning of the entire LDM stage depends on — adopting it would have meant redesigning the denoiser from scratch.

Cost and verdict

Eight runs, about one week. Closed as a documented negative result; the spatial KL bottleneck stays in production.

Caveat

Even after both bug fixes, VRRAE reconstruction sits about 3× worse than the spatial baselines. The comparison is not fully like-for-like — the VRRAE variant has no mask head and no discriminator — so this refutes the bottleneck as a drop-in replacement here, not the method in general.

04 · Conditioning design Design principle

Conditioning must be compositional,
never categorical.

A one-hot "specimen family" input can only ever reproduce the three materials in the dataset. A new stacking sequence would be an unseen class — impossible by construction.

A continuous orientation field instead lets the model learn local orientation + local porosity → texture + voids. A novel layup then becomes a recombination of parts it has already seen.

Stated goal

Generate arbitrary stacking sequences. Whether an unseen layup generates well is an open empirical question — it is out of distribution by definition. But this encoding is what makes the question askable at all.

What follows

Nine measurements, T-A to T-I, decide which signals enter the conditioning vector and how each one is encoded. None of them was chosen by intuition.

Real, and z-only

The per-volume z peak falls inside 18–22 voxels for 70% of 80 volumes; the same band fires in y or x for only 1.3%. Median excess: 25.2× in z against 4.5× and 4.0×. Correlation lengths run 79 voxels in z to 901 in x — 1.2 to 14 patch widths.

Confirmed twice, independently

Voxel-level autocorrelation has a secondary peak at lag exactly 20. Separately, porosity maxima coincide with ply interfaces: correlation +0.257 at zero lag with the orientation change rate, positive in 85% of volumes. The periodicity is ply-interface driven.

The design consequence

A 64-voxel patch spans ≈3.3 plies. One angle per patch would erase the ply sequence inside the patch — which is exactly the structure we want to control. Orientation must be depth-resolved, and neighbour conditioning is justified by the r values on the right.

Caveat

The patch index cannot see any of this: stride 32 puts the Nyquist limit at 64 voxels, above the ply pitch. It took an exact 1-voxel pass over all 80 volumes to find. Narrow spectral lines also fire in y and x in 58–65% of volumes at scan-dependent periods — per-scan artefacts that vanish under median stacking.

How to ask for a porosity

04 · ConditioningT-E · T-F
T-E · p(local φ | global φ). Ratio p10 = 0.109, p50 = 0.839, p90 = 1.738 — a factor of 16 inside one volume.
T-F · effective levels out of 256 for each candidate transform of φ.
Never paint the volume target uniformly

Only 12.9% of real patches sit within 0.001 of their own volume's mean porosity, and only 53.4% within 0.005. Painting one global number into every patch's local channel asks for a configuration the data almost never contains — and it was the source of the local/global conflation bug in the earlier conditioning code. At inference we instead sample one coherent field over the volume, smooth it to the T-D correlation lengths, and rescale its mean to the requested target.

log(φ + 1e-3), not raw φ

Raw φ resolves 74 of 256 levels and puts the median at 5% of the input range. The log transform gives 227 levels and moves the median to 42%. A quantile transform scores marginally higher at 248 — and was rejected: the log is analytic, invertible, extrapolates beyond the training range, keeps its physical meaning, and is not tied to this dataset, which matters for the planned public-data transfer.

Caveat

Porosity control is confounded with material family in this dataset — Nacho at 0.0223 against Pegaso at 0.0121. The conditioning has to let the model tell them apart, or a dose-response curve will show a mixture bias that looks exactly like a conditioning bug.

Three encoding rules

Pool the components, never the angle — averaging θ is wrong across the 180° wrap. Do not renormalise the pooled vector: where a plane straddles an interface, the shrinkage is the interface signal. Build θ(z) from the nominal sequence aligned to the scan, never from measured residuals.

Deliberately dropped

Global volume porosity (redundant once the field sets the mean, and the source of a real bug). In-plane x/y position (nothing varies with it). The 4θ term (one nominal angle per ply makes 2θ sufficient). Measured waviness — that is material variation for the model to generate, not a control input.

Caveat

The 84.7% is oracle-aligned relative agreement, not autonomous accuracy: the global rotation offset and hypothesis selection use the nominal labels. The estimator is validated for one coarse angle per ply on this layup — not as a local orientation estimator, and not for unidirectional laminates. The original justification, "the volume mean is isotropic", is also false; the correct description is within-specimen common-mode contrast.

ε-MSE is not comparable across the μ and μ+σε targets: ldm02's 0.48 against ldm01's 0.20 is expected, not a regression. A per-timestep breakdown shows the gap is the irreducible σ·ε floor at low t, given the VAE's σ ≈ 0.87 — not a normalisation bug.

The neighbours were handing the model the answer

05 · DiffusionD38
1 · The measurement that condemns it

With neighbours at offset 32, the +z neighbour reproduces the target's overlapping region at MAE 0.067, against 0.744 for a random patch — a ratio of 0.09. The neighbour latent was very nearly a literal copy of half the target.

Healthy, at offset 640.86
2 · And the coverage was total

Opposite neighbours invert the same parity bit, so they always fall in the same group and always arrive together. One opposite pair tiles the target completely: −z supplies latent cells 0–7, +z supplies 8–15, across the full y and x extent. Result: 7 of the 8 parity groups received the target's entire content. The model would learn to copy, not to generate.

3 · How it was caught

By a loss that was too good. The contaminated ldm05 run hit val 0.497 at step 1000 — already below ldm04's best-ever 0.524 at step 36k. The first reading was "the conditioning is informative." The information was the answer.

The fix, and what it costs

neighbour_offset = 64 — neighbours now touch, sharing zero voxels. generation_stride = 64 — exact tiling, no overlap, and therefore no blending. sample_stride stays at 32, since dataset density was only ever a data multiplier. No dataset rebuild was needed: neighbours at ±64 are two steps of the existing stride-32 grid and were already in the store. This repealed two of the three assembly fixes decided two days earlier — both solved problems that only exist when neighbours overlap.

Why removing the blending is an improvement

Blending conceals disagreement rather than resolving it. With exact tiling, continuity rests entirely on the learned conditioning, and seam quality becomes a measured quantity instead of a disguised artefact.

Caveat

The tempting alternative — a denser grid with averaged overlaps — is unsound for the same reason: the mean of two independent unit-variance latents has variance ½, so every seam would feed the decoder systematically shrunken latents. It also multiplies the dataset by 8 (≈18 M patches, ≈860 GB) and does not fix the leak.

Trained conditioning was not enough for invisible seams

06 · Sampler
During ldm05 training the seam diagnostic did something unwelcome: as the model matured, seams got worse — grayscale 1.71 → 2.96 between steps 40k and 80k. Interiors sharpen; joints do not.
The joint sampler

Overlapping windows at stride 32 denoise jointly: ε predictions are fused on one shared latent canvas at every DDIM step, and the canvas is decoded once at the end on the stride-64 tiling grid. This is MultiDiffusion's mechanism, applied inside the reverse process — inference-time only, no retraining, exactly as pre-registered.

Why fusing predictions and not outputs

Averaging decoded patches, or averaging latents, is unsound: the mean of two independent unit-variance latents has variance ½, which is systematically off-manifold — the same defect class as the decode-scale bug. Averaging the model's ε predictions inside the reverse process is the principled form.

The OOB hypothesis is refuted

ldm06: teach the model where material is

08 · OutlookD40

1 · Labelled material map

onlypores already returns a material boundary. Store it per patch at latent resolution and feed it as an input channel. At inference it is painted: all-material for interiors, "no material here" outside the specimen, arbitrary shapes as a new generative control.

2 · Mask-aware auxiliary loss

ε-MSE is blind to air and to φ. Add an auxiliary term in decoded x̂₀ space at low t: air outside the material map, |mask mean − conditioned φ|, grayscale/mask agreement, and the real patch mask. Same objective, reweighted in the space that matters. Cost ≈1.3–1.5× per step.

3 · Neighbours for joint mode

Today the trained neighbour channels have no valid input in joint mode, so they are set all-UNKNOWN. Train them with random corruption at a declared noise level, then feed them at inference from the canvas x̂₀ at ±64 voxels, with a per-neighbour descriptor.

4 · Directional distances

Replace the single distance-to-surface scalar with six per-face distances, consistent with the OOB flags — so "which way is the outside" is expressible, not just "how far".

Pre-registered gates — written before the run, not after

Unmasked interior air ≤2% at 192³ and ≤8% at 1024 scale (against 15% / 38% today). Mask pore capture ≥0.8 in cells with ≤5% air (against 0.3–0.5). No regression on E2: local dose-response R² ≥0.95, unseen layups ≤5° median. Budget ≈2.5 GPU-days for the base run, 7–8 days including the two ablations.

Pre-training probe: 150 high-air and 93 low-air cells re-encoded through the frozen VAE.
The probe already says something useful

High-air cells are not wilder — std ratio 0.98 against 1.08 — but they are displaced: per-channel mean shift 0.383 against 0.178 in train-std units, Cohen's d = 1.17. One channel carries almost all of it, correlating with dark-air fraction at r = 0.956. The failure is a mean shift in a specific latent direction, not a variance blow-up — which is what makes a targeted conditioning and loss fix plausible.

Then

DDIM step sweep {50, 100, 200, 300} to separate discretisation from model error · baselines (SliceGAN, pixel DDPM, Naiff 2025) · downstream 3D U-Net synthetic-to-real transfer · Paper 1 to IEEE TIP, November 2026.

What T-G got right

Ply interfaces coincide with porosity maxima: the porosity profile correlates with the orientation change rate at +0.257 at zero lag, positive in 85% of volumes, and 98% of the strong cases peak within 3 voxels of zero lag or one ply pitch. The resolution limit, stated honestly

Individual fibres are not resolved: a 7 µm fibre is about one voxel at 25 µm. Anisotropy grows with scale — 0.071 at 8–16 voxels, 0.188 at 16–64, 0.333 at 64–256 — so the signal is tow-level: tow bands and resin channels, not fibres.

The artefact control T-H ran

The discrete FFT puts kx = 0 and ky = 0 into single angular bins, producing a one-bin spike at exactly 0° and 90° in every volume. Excised; ±1° against ±3° excision moves the coefficients by under 8%. Appendix · layup extraction, detail

BackupT-I
Measured against expert ground truth, per ply, all 78 usable volumes.
Four-class confusion. +45 against −45 confuse at 3.8% / 4.6%; 0 against 90 at 4.9% / 0.6%.
The feature definition, for the paper

Per volume, two 1024×1024 in-plane windows at 30% and 70% of the y extent, centred in x, per z-slice. Each window is mean-removed, Hann-windowed, 2D FFT. Power is binned into 12 log-radial × 180 angular bins, each divided by its pixel count so there is no square-grid bias, and the angular axis is rotated 90° into the real-space texture frame. The band used is 0.82–3.27 × the volume's own measured ply pitch — per-volume scaled, not fixed.

The residual is real material, not error

The two windows are independent measurements of the same ply, so they give a built-in noise estimate. Nacho's larger residual is shared between windows at r = +0.60 — 71% real variance — so Nacho genuinely has about twice Pegaso's ply misalignment, 3.6° against 1.8° standard deviation. Within-ply deviation has lag-1 autocorrelation +0.38, positive in 99% of volumes: spatially coherent fibre waviness. That is why waviness is left for the model to generate rather than being used as a control input.

Negatives worth recording

Slab-averaging inside a ply costs 12 accuracy points — do not do it. Radon adds nothing (it is the Fourier-slice dual of the same FFT). The four _cleaned Pegaso volumes score 0.650 against 0.885: that preprocessing variant damages the signal, and whether to exclude them is still open. Layup A is near-palindromic, so a wrong face ordering changes only two plies and a wrong sign convention swaps all ±45 — the thin fit margins are largely a symmetry artefact.

How the metric is defined

Mean absolute slice-to-slice difference at the patch-boundary planes, divided by the same quantity at interior planes. EMA 0.9999, and φ requests clamped to the observed data range [0.002, 0.107]: no extrapolation outside training support.

Convergence protocol

Every 20k steps, roughly one minute of wall clock, reading latest.ckpt from disk with no shared state with the training process.

Raw and EMA weights — raw answers "is it learning", EMA answers "what would we ship".

Split by neighbour-context bucket 0/2/4/6, n = 16 each, pooled 1/3/3/1.

Automatic verdict appended to a JSONL: CONVERGING if the overall std-ratio fell ≥5% since the last check, STALLED within ±5%, REGRESSING if it rose ≥5%, with per-bucket deltas so a stall in one context cannot hide in the average.

Plus a conditioning-alive check at fixed seed, and an on-demand input-contribution probe — the latter was used to refute the input-dilution hypothesis: the first convolution weights the 4 latent channels about 10× above any conditioning group, and zeroing the neighbours moves the output by 37% of the noise scale.

Campaign caveats, verbatim from the record

1. Real masks are annotations and miss some dark features (drilled holes, edge effects), so pooled Dice ≈0.8 is a ceiling, not a shortfall.

2. The corrected_porosity values written into the per-volume stats.json during phases 1–2 use an earlier, over-inclusive material-referenced threshold. Use the audit's recalibrated per-volume numbers instead.

3. Layup generalisation is demonstrated, not validated: 74 of 78 training volumes share layup A.

4. Three seeds per cell, two for layup. Seed spread is reported; the distributions are not tight enough for fine per-cell ranking.

5. Direct angle scores use the frozen identity convention; fitted variants live in the per-volume layup records.
